# SHazTunez - closed test invite

Send to people who might test.  Before they open the link: add their
Play Store Google account email to the `tunez` list (Closed testing ›
Alpha › Testers), and their country to Alpha › Countries / regions if
they're outside the US.  Production needs 12 testers opted in for 14
days straight.

---

**Subject: Want to help test my music app?**

Hi!

I wrote an Android music player called **SHazTunez**, and I'm putting it
on Google Play. Before Google lets me release it publicly, I need **12
people to test it for 14 days**. Could you be one of them?

**What it does:** it plays the music files already on your phone (mp3,
flac, ogg, wav) from one folder. There are no ads, no accounts and no
streaming. You rate songs however you like, pick which ratings to hear,
and shuffle never repeats a song until you've heard everything you
picked. It can also cast to Chromecast or Google Cast speakers.

More info: https://shaz.app/tunez/

**What you'd need:**
- An Android phone (no iPhones, sorry)
- The Gmail / Google account email your phone's Play Store uses.
  **Reply with it** and I'll add you to the tester list.
- Some music files of your own

**How to join, once I've added you:**
1. Open this link on your phone:
   **https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.shaz.tunez**
2. Tap **Become a tester**, then **Download it on Google Play**, then
   Install.
3. Open SHazTunez and tap **Pick folder**. It makes a `Music/tunez`
   folder and opens it. Tap **Use this folder**, then **Allow**.
4. Copy some songs into `Music/tunez` (from a computer over USB works
   fine), then come back to the app.

**About file names:** the app keeps each song's rating at the end of its
file name, like ``Artist ` Title ` a.mp3``. You don't need to rename
anything. Songs with other names get tidied up automatically and rated
`new`. Tap **rate** to rate the song that's playing.

**The important part:** please **keep the app installed and stay opted
in for at least 14 days**. If people drop out, Google's 14-day clock
starts over. You don't have to use it every day, but I'd love to hear
about anything that breaks, confuses you, or that you like. Just email
me at sh@shaz.app.

Thanks!
Steve
